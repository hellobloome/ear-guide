# Bloomé Bahasa Melayu Style Guide

Tone: clear, calm, professional and conversational.

Preferred patterns:
- `Apakah yang ingin anda fokuskan hari ini?`
- `Mulakan di sini`
- `Gabungan titik yang dicadangkan`
- `Tekan perlahan`
- `Tanggalkan ear seed jika...`
- `Dapatkan rawatan profesional jika...`

Product terminology:
- Keep `ear seed` in English as the product name.
- Use `titik telinga` for acupoint in general consumer-facing copy.
- Keep technical anatomical terms in point-location descriptions where useful.

Avoid:
- word-for-word English sentence structure
- excessive `reset`
- overly formal bureaucratic Malay
- medical treatment or cure language
