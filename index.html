About Bloomé

Wellness guidance, made easier to understand.

Bloomé creates approachable self-care tools and education for everyday wellness. This guide is for general informational use and does not replace professional medical advice.

Important

Bloomé Guide provides general wellness education only. Ear seeds are not a substitute for diagnosis or medical treatment. Remove them if irritation, swelling, dizziness or significant discomfort occurs. Keep seeds away from the ear canal and seek professional care for persistent or severe symptoms.
